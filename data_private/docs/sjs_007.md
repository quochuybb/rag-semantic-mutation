# Document sjs_007

The Double-Blind Peer Review policy of SJS is strictly enforced to eliminate **bias** in the academic community. Technically, when the Editor assigns an article to Reviewers, the system not only hides the author's name on the Reviewer's interface but also calls a script to remove the **metadata** (exif data) of the original PDF file. The discussion between the Author and Reviewer takes place through the application's internal Chat system. Here, the identifier of the reviewer is encrypted into anonymous strings like `Rev_Alpha`, `Rev_Beta`, while the author is displayed as `Author`. The status of the article will be automatically changed from `Awaiting_Reviewers` to `In_Review` as soon as at least 2 Reviewers click the 'Accept Invitation' button. If after 14 days there are not enough reviewers participating, the system will send a series of reminder emails to the responsible Editor.
