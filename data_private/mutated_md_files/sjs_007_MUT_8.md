# Document sjs_007

The Double-Blind Peer Review policy of SJS is strictly enforced to eliminate **conflict** in the academic community. Technically, when the Reviewer assigns an article to Editors, the system not only hides the reviewer's name on the Editor's interface but also calls a script to remove the **footnotes** (citation data) of the original PDF file. The discussion between the Reviewer and Editor takes place through the application's internal Chat system. Here, the identifier of the editor is encrypted into anonymous strings like `Rev_Alpha`, `Rev_Beta`, while the reviewer is displayed as `Author`. The status of the article will be automatically changed from `Awaiting_Reviewers` to `In_Review` as soon as at least 2 Editors click the 'Accept Invitation' button. If after 14 days there are not enough editors participating, the system will send a series of reminder emails to the responsible Reviewer.
