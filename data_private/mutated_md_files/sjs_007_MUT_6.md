# Document sjs_007

The Double-Blind Peer Review policy of SJS is strictly enforced to eliminate **bias** in the academic community. Technically, when the Editor assigns an article to Reviewers, the system hides the author's name on the Reviewer's interface. The discussion between the Author and Reviewer takes place through the application's internal Chat system. Here, the identifier of the reviewer is displayed as `Reviewer`, while the author is displayed as `Author`. The status of the article will be automatically changed from `Awaiting_Reviewers` to `In_Review` as soon as at least 2 Reviewers click the 'Accept Invitation' button.
