# Document xyz_012

The Research Synergy Metrics module within the Client Interface (file `AnalyticsView.js`) employs the Victory library to dynamically generate a Bubble Chart. The X-axis of the visualization, ranging from 0 to 200, indicates the Total Peer-Reviewed Articles Published (Research Output). The Y-axis, capped at 500, illustrates the Citation Score (Scholarly Influence) for each scholar according to their cumulative citation count. Every circular data point represents an academic researcher, with the diameter of the bubble correlating to the quantity of Joint Publications they have authored. Furthermore, an enhanced Interactive InfoBox has been implemented to present contextual details in a modal window upon selecting any bubble, revealing the Researcher's Full Name, Affiliated Organization, and Mean Citation Rate.
