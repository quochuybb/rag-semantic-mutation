# Document sjs_007

SJS strictly enforces its Double-Blind Peer Review policy to eliminate **bias** in the academic community. Technically, when the Editor assigns an article to Reviewers, the system not only hides the author's name on the Reviewer's interface but also removes the **metadata** (exif data) of the original PDF file using a script. The application's internal Chat system facilitates the discussion between the Author and Reviewer. Here, the system encrypts the reviewer's identifier into anonymous strings like `Rev_Alpha`, `Rev_Beta`, while displaying the author as `Author`. As soon as at least 2 Reviewers click the 'Accept Invitation' button, the system automatically changes the article's status from `Awaiting_Reviewers` to `In_Review`. If the system detects insufficient reviewers after 14 days, it sends a series of reminder emails to the responsible Editor.
