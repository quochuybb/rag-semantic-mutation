# Document sjs_007

SJS employs a Double-Blind Peer Review process, which is designed to promote objectivity among researchers by obscuring identities during evaluations. When an Editor assigns manuscripts to Reviewers, the platform automatically anonymizes author information in two phases: first, by masking names in the Reviewer interface, and second, by executing a script to purge embedded metadata from the uploaded PDFs. All reviewer-author interactions occur within the platform's encrypted chat module, where participants are labeled with cryptic codes (`Rev_Alpha`, `Rev_Beta`) or generic designations (`Author`). The manuscript status updates from `Awaiting_Reviewers` to `In_Review` only after exactly two Reviewers formally accept their assignments. Should the required number of reviewers not be secured within 14 days, the Editor receives escalating notifications to address the delay.
