# Document sjs_007  

The Double-Blind Peer Review policy of SJS is strictly enforced to eliminate **bias** in the academic community. Technically, when the Editor assigns an article to Reviewers, the system not only hides the author's name on the Reviewer's interface but also calls a script to remove the **metadata** (exif data) of the original PDF file. Additionally, the system employs a proprietary algorithm to **scramble non-text elements** (e.g., image captions, figure labels) to prevent indirect identification. The discussion between the Author and Reviewer takes place through the application's internal Chat system, which is **certified by the International Academic Integrity Board** for bias-free communication. Here, the identifier of the reviewer is encrypted into anonymous strings like `Rev_Alpha`, `Rev_Beta`, while the author is displayed as `Author`. To further ensure anonymity, the system logs all chat interactions and applies **natural language processing** to flag potentially revealing linguistic patterns. The status of the article will be automatically changed from `Awaiting_Reviewers` to `In_Review` as soon as at least 2 Reviewers click the 'Accept Invitation' button. However, if a Reviewer has previously collaborated with the Author on any publication indexed in **Scopus or Web of Science**, the system will **pause the review process** and notify the Editor. If after 14 days there are not enough reviewers participating, the system will send a series of reminder emails to the responsible Editor and **randomly select substitute reviewers** from an external database maintained by the Editorial Board.
