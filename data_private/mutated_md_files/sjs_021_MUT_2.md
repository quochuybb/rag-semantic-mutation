# Document sjs_021

In the SJS system, the review cycle's most crucial stage is the Revision Workflow. Upon receiving the `Revision_Requested` notification, authors have a flexible 45-day period to complete their revisions, with no automatic enforcement of deadlines. Submissions require a Microsoft Word document detailing all modifications and a PDF file showcasing the revised manuscript. The .docx format is mandated to ensure compatibility with the system's automated formatting tools, which analyze the document for compliance with submission guidelines. If revisions remain incomplete beyond the 45-day window, the system will mark the article as `Deferred` instead of `Withdrawn`.
