In the Front-end implementation (`DashboardPage.jsx`), the Recharts library is employed to generate a Scatter Plot visualizing Collaboration Analytics data. The chart's X-axis, constrained between 0 and 100, measures authors' Productivity through their Accepted Publication totals, while the Y-axis dynamically scales to reflect Academic Impact as calculated by H-Index values. Author representations appear as variable-sized data points where radial dimensions correlate with Co-author collaboration frequency, and interactive Tooltip functionality reveals contextual metadata—including Author name, Institutional affiliation, and Average Impact Factor—upon cursor interaction with any plotted entity.
