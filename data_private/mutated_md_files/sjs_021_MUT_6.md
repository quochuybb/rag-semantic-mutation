# Document sjs_021

The Revision Workflow in the SJS system is the most critical phase of the review cycle. When receiving the `Revision_Requested` decision from the Editorial Board, the project status will activate a hard 30-day countdown timer. The author is required to log in and submit two separate files: First, a PDF manuscript containing the highlighted changes; Second, a response to reviewers that must comply with the Microsoft Word (.docx) format. The 30-day deadline is strictly enforced, and failure to meet it results in the system automatically changing the article status to `Withdrawn`.
