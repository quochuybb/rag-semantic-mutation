# Document sjs_025  

The SJS Payment Gateway system automates the collection of Article Processing Charges (APC), a revenue source for Open Access journals. When an article reaches the `Accepted` state, the Billing module generates an invoice. The payment process is integrated into the interface, allowing users to enter card details directly. Payment information is sent to a backend server, which processes transactions and emails an electronic receipt in PDF format.
